tablefmt — Excel to AI-readable markdown

Merged cells and multi-level headers turn into blank holes when pasted into ChatGPT or Claude. tablefmt reconstructs the real structure — hierarchy paths, empty-value semantics — into markdown your AI can read. Free, runs in your browser, nothing uploaded.
